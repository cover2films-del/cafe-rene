My René offers an explicit Explore Daniel’s demo button; an existing account is not silently switched. Daniel's sample dataset is seeded once transactionally. Edits, reversals and deletion are not overwritten on restart.

## Groups and messaging

Circles are private by construction. Create a named group or a one-to-one invitation, search only discoverable display names, invite a member, and have that member accept/decline in their own account. No email address, consumption history or reward balance is exposed by discovery. New customer accounts are not discoverable by default. The seeded demo profiles opt in so the workflow is easy to try.

Only active members can read/send messages or view plans. Invited members cannot read a conversation until they join. Only the owner can invite to a group; a direct conversation cannot be expanded into a group. Staff/manager roles do not bypass group membership. Messages are escaped plain text, length/rate limited and idempotent by client UUID. Plans have a future date/time, notes and a single per-member yes/maybe/no RSVP. Creating a plan does not reserve a table or buy tickets.

The sample Friday group has Daniel plus fictional Demo Alex and Demo Sam, three explicitly marked example messages and a sample plan. To try invitations, sign in as Daniel, message Demo Alex, then sign in as demo-alex@demo.rene to join and reply. Browser login includes demo-account shortcuts; native login accepts these emails.

Conversations refresh while visible (about ten seconds), not through push notifications. No external email, SMS, Slack or Meta messages are sent. The app does not claim end-to-end encryption: messages are stored on the server with access controls. Account export includes that member’s authored messages and memberships. Account deletion removes membership and discovery, removes authored plans and anonymises authored shared messages; remaining members retain their own conversation context.

This is a private demo, not a production social network. Before a public launch, replace shared demo accounts, define moderation/block/report workflows and retention, and complete account/device security testing.
